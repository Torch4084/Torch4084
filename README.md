### ⚡ About Me

<img align="right" width="240" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Torch4084/Torch4084/main/torch-walk-walk.gif" alt="Torch Mascot" />

- 🛡️ **Cybersecurity & CTFs**: Active competitor on the **JordanSec** CTF team, specializing in **Web Exploitation** and **OSINT**. Driven by vulnerability research and deconstructing complex web application environments.
- 🐧 **Linux & Systems**: Passionate Linux power user (**Arch** & **Fedora**). I spend substantial time optimizing system architecture, scripting custom **Hyprland** window manager configurations, and exploring kernel-level tuning.
- 🚀 **Open Source Projects**: Creator of [**cleanroom**](https://github.com/Torch4084/cleanroom) (a lightweight GTK4 GUI for managing `systemd-nspawn` containers without Docker) and [**hypr-control**](https://github.com/Torch4084/hypr-control).
- 🎮 **Game Design & 3D**: Exploring game development and mechanics in **Unity (C#)**, combined with 3D modeling and asset creation in **Blender** & **Autodesk Maya**.
- 🤖 **AI Tinkering**: Experimenting with local LLMs, autonomous agent workflows, and command-line AI tooling.
- 💬 **Connect with Me**: Hit me up on Discord (`Torch4084`) or read my technical writeups on [**t0rch.xyz**](https://t0rch.xyz).
